COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Here are answers to some common questions about getting started.

How do I start planning my event? +

Begin by identifying your event type, preferred date, approximate budget and the services you need. You can then visit our Plan Your Event page to organize your requirements.

What types of events can I explore? +

You can explore ideas for weddings, corporate gatherings, private celebrations and other occasions using the service and resource sections of this website.

Can event service providers get in touch? +

Yes. Select Vendor Enquiry in the contact form and include information about your services. The form must be connected to a working email or backend before enquiries can be received by the website owner.

Does submitting this demo form send my message? +

Not yet. This page is a front-end demo. Connect the form to a backend or form handling service to send and store messages.
