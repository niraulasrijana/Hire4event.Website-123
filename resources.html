PLAN WITH CONFIDENCE

Build a Smarter Event Budget

Start with these common expense categories and adjust them according to your event requirements.

01

Venue and Setup

Consider venue rental, seating, stage setup, lighting and any required equipment.

02

Food and Beverages

Estimate the number of guests and confirm menu, service and dietary requirements.

03

Vendors and Entertainment

Account for photography, decoration, music, performers and other service providers.

04

Contingency

Keep a reserve for unexpected expenses and last-minute changes.

These are planning categories, not quoted prices. Actual costs depend on location, guest count, dates and selected services.

What to Check Before Booking a Venue

  • Capacity and seating arrangements
  • Location and guest accessibility

COMMON QUESTIONS

Event Planning FAQs

When should I start planning an event?

It depends on the size and complexity of your event. Larger events may require several months of planning, while smaller gatherings can need less time. Book important services as early as practical.

How do I decide on an event budget?

Begin with your priorities, estimated guest count and available funds. List expected expenses and keep some money aside for unexpected costs.

Which vendors might I need?

Depending on the event, you may need a venue, caterer, photographer, decorator, sound and lighting team, entertainer or event planner.

How can I keep an event organized?

Maintain a checklist, confirm bookings in writing, assign responsibilities and share a clear schedule with everyone involved.
